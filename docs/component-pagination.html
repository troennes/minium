Pagination

Pagination is a group of controls that lets users move between pages of related content.

Usage

  • Wrap the pagination controls in <nav aria-label="Pagination">.
  • Place the controls in an unclassed <ul>.
  • Add aria-current="page" to the control for the current page.
  • Add aria-disabled="true" to disabled controls.

Accessibility notes

  • To remove a disabled control from the tab order, add tabindex="-1". The aria-disabled="true" attribute alone does not prevent focus or activation.
