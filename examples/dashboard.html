    Your workspace becomes read-only until you choose a plan. Your reports and data remain available.

    Can I change plans later?

    Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your billing settings.

    No. Your reports stay in your workspace while you decide which plan is right for your team.

    How does billing work?
