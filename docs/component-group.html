Group

A group joins related elements into a single connected control or visual set. Depending on its contents, the group shares borders, rounds only its outer corners, or overlaps its children.

Usage

  • Add role="group" to the container. Use a <fieldset> for form controls and a <div> for other grouped elements.
  • Use children of similar height, such as inputs, selects, and buttons. Mixed heights prevent their borders from aligning.
  • Groups do not stack on small viewports. For responsive stacking, use Switcher instead.

Accessibility notes

  • A visual group has no accessible name by default. Add an aria-label when assistive technologies need to announce the group’s purpose, as shown by aria-label="Text alignment".
  • Give each control a distinct accessible name that describes its action.
